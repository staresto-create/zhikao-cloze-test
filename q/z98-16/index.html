The dowry system, money or property brought by a bride to her husband at marriage, was started centuries ago with the intention of providing security for a girl 16 difficulties and unexpected circumstances after marriage.

  1. (A)due to
  2. (B)apart from
  3. (C)in case of
  4. (D)with reference to

答案：(C)　in case of

題目編號：Z98_16（已自動幫你填到回報表單裡）🚩 回報這題有問題

解析

in case of 意為「以防、萬一發生…時」：嫁妝制度原是為了讓女子在婚後遇到困難或突發狀況時有所保障；due to（由於）、apart from（除…之外）、with reference to（關於）語意都不合。

深入解析

你可能會這樣想

四個選項都是幾個字合起來當一個介系詞用（劍橋 English Grammar Today 的 Prepositions 條目），中文翻起來都通順，只能靠語意的方向判斷。in case of ＝ 萬一發生…時，帶有「預先防範」的意味：In case of fire, use the stairs.（萬一失火，請走樓梯）。

線索是 security

空格前面是 providing security for a girl（為女子提供保障）——既然是「保障」，後面接的必然是「萬一遇到什麼狀況」，這兩個概念天生配成一對。「看到 security、protection、insurance 這類字，後面常接 in case of 或 against。」

誘答選項

due to 是「由於」，會變成「因為困難而提供保障」，因果顛倒——保障是預先給的，不是事後補的；apart from 是「除…之外」；with reference to 是「關於」。

in case 的兩種用法

in case of + 名詞（萬一發生…）；in case + 子句（以防…）：Take an umbrella in case it rains.（帶把傘以防下雨）。

常考字詞與句型

in case of fire／emergency（萬一失火／發生緊急狀況）、in case + 子句（以防…）、provide security for（為…提供保障）、insure against（為…投保）。

In spite of modernization and the increasing role of women in all walks of life, the practice of the dowry in India is still widespread. The dowry system, money or property brought by a bride to her husband at marriage, was started centuries ago with the intention of providing security for a girl 16 difficulties and unexpected circumstances after marriage.

For this purpose, the parents gave 17 they could to their daughter, which consequently went to the groom's family. By the beginning of the 21st century, however, the custom had deteriorated to a point whereby the groom and his family had become very 18.

When demands for dowry are not met, the bride is 19 torture, and often even killed. The more educated a man is, the 20 is the expectation for dowry at the time of marriage. Girls who are highly educated are required to have larger dowries because they usually marry more educated men.

中譯：儘管社會現代化、女性在各行各業的角色日益提升，印度的嫁妝習俗至今依然十分普遍。嫁妝制度——即新娘結婚時帶到夫家的金錢或財產——起源於幾百年前，最初的用意是為女兒婚後可能遭遇的困難與意外狀況提供保障。基於這個目的，父母會把自己能給的一切都給女兒，而這些財產最終會歸入夫家。然而到了 21 世紀初，這項習俗已經惡化到一種地步，使得新郎與其家人變得非常貪婪。當嫁妝要求沒有被滿足時，新娘便可能遭受凌虐，甚至被殺害。一個男性受教育程度越高，結婚時對嫁妝的期望往往也越高。因此，受過高等教育的女孩通常需要準備更豐厚的嫁妝，因為她們往往會嫁給教育程度更高的男性。

  2. It began as security for a bride, her parents giving what they could to the groom's family.
  3. By this century grooms and their families demanded far more; unmet demands bring torture or death.
